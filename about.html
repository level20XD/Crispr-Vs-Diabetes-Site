About this project

The purpose of this project is to spread awareness about Crispr technology, and specifically spread awareness on how it is being used to develop a cure for type 1 diabetes. This information about the applications of Crispr gene-editing is very important, and so it is important that more people know about it. My intended audience for this project are people who have type 1 diabetes who are interested in developments of treatment, people who are interested in gene-editing technology and want to know about how it can be used, and people interested in recent breakthroughs of medical technology in general.


My goal is to advocate for Crispr development, and to show how beneficial it could be to the field of medicine, and how it could change diabetics' lives for the better. Through this website I plan to show this by giving an overview on how Crispr works, and show that much progress has already been made so it’s just a matter of time before the Crispr diabetic treatment can be available to the public. Since my intended audience is closer to the general public rather than medical professionals, I decided to try to describe things in simple ways so that even people who do not have a strong grasp in biology could understand. I tried to avoid any jargon and complex terms and instead explained things through familiar concepts. Still, I would recommend having at least a surface level understanding of biology for some parts.


One thing you may have noticed is that this website is more focused on type 1 diabetic treatment than type 2. There are two main reasons why I have made this decision. The first reason is that type 2 diabetes is currently much more treatable than type 1, as it can be managed through lifestyle changes and does not necessarily require daily insulin injections, unlike type 1. On top of that, getting type 2 diabetes can be somewhat avoided before it happens, while type 1 cannot. The second reason why is that someone very important in my life has type 1 diabetes, and I have seen first hand how much it has impacted their life. The idea that Crispr is developing a cure for type 1 diabetes is exciting for me, and I hope it could help change their life for the better.


From this website, what I want for my viewers to get out of this is an optimistic feeling about the future of medicine and science as a whole. Scientists have been struggling with finding a cure for diabetes for decades, so the fact that we are so close to inventing this cure to the point where we are having test trials is extremely promising. And with how fast medicine is advancing now, who knows what’s next to come? Crispr technologies have tons of potential with curing varieties of diseases, and it has already proven itself useful in several applications. I would also want my viewers to get a heightened interest in medical science and biology. Scientists are working very hard to create medicine that can help people all over the world, and the work they do is very important, so it is also important that we can appreciate all of their efforts.
